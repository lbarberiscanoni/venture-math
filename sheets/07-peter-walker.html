3 · The exit door

Most leavers get 90 days to pay up, and most walk away

Share of vested options exercised before they expired, by quarter

~80%of leavers got 90 days or less, 2023–Q1 24 (83–91% a quarter, 2018–22)
77%of vested grants went back to the pool, Q3 2024 (low: 52%, Q4 2021)
33%exercised even when in the money, Q3 2024 (55% at the 2021 peak)
54%of entry-level staff exercised none of their in-the-money options (2022)

Why 90 days: an ISO not exercised within 3 months of leaving becomes an NSO (IRC §422). A longer window is legal; it just changes the tax. 62% of companies give 90 days, 10% longer, 28% tie it to tenure.

Who gives longer windows: 10 years — Quora, Notion, Supabase, PostHog, Flexport; Linear, Fireworks AI (after 2 yrs); Gusto (after 3). 7 years — Brex, Patreon, Netlify (after 2 yrs); Pinterest*, Coinbase*. Carta: 1 year per year vested.

5 · Seed-strapping, up close

A fantastic way to build a business, a bad way to get venture returns

~50% / 30% / 4%reach Series A with no bridge / after a priced bridge / after a SAFE or note bridge
~1 in 2acquired startups had raised under $10M; under 10% had raised $100M+
74%of US VC-backed exits were acquisitions, 2025 through May
$1.3M → $100M ARRZapier: one seed (2012), profitable by 2014, $100M ARR by 2020
