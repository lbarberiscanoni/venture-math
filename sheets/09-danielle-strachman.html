Who founders are — the age and origin the Fellowship bets against, and what a Thiel co-founder keptTEAL = UNDER 25

Unicorn founders peak at 30–34. Teenagers are almost absent.

Age at founding, 1,345 founders of 523 US unicorns (1997–2019). Only 8 started at 18–19, the Fellowship’s core age.

Ilya Strebulaev, Stanford GSB Venture Capital Initiative (10/2021)

44% of US unicorn founders weren’t born in the US

Founders born abroad, top 10 countries; 1,078 founders of 500 US unicorns (1997–2019). Israel also founds US unicorns at ~10× a matched random sample (29 vs. 3).

Ilya Strebulaev, Stanford GSB Venture Capital Initiative (01/2022; 10/2022)

Figma: the co-founder left, the stake stayed

Evan Wallace co-founded Figma with Dylan Field (Thiel 2012) and left around 2021 (his site: “2012–2021”). Value of his family trust’s Class B stake. It sold 0 shares in the IPO.

Plus two gifts: 13.4M shares to a charity in June 2025, sold in the IPO for $441M (donor named only by Fortune), and 4.4M in June 2026 (~$90M).

Figma 424B4, 13D/A No. 2, 2026 proxy · Adobe 424B3 (Jan 2023) · prices: IPO Scoop, stockanalysis

…and Field votes it

Share of Figma’s votes: Field’s own · Wallace’s, via proxy · everyone else. Class B carries 15 votes a share.

A 2022 irrevocable proxy (Field paid $250,000) lets him vote the trust’s shares “in his sole discretion”, with no end date. Shares Wallace sells convert to 1-vote Class A and leave the proxy, so a full sale would leave Field ~61% on his own.
