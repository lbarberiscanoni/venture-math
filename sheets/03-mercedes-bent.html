3×
The institutional transition, in one line: the A prices at about three times the seed, sells about the same slice of the company — and takes the founding team below 50% for good. What changes is not the dilution. It is the board seat, the pro-rata, and the bar you now have to clear every quarter. Discussion — Mercedes Bent, Premise VC
