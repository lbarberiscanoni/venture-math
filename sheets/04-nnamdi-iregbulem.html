40%
Why the funnel narrows here: the investor’s job changes. Nnamdi’s argument — building on AngelList’s return data — is that seed investors should index: back every credible deal, because the cost of missing one winner is unbounded. Later-stage investors have track records to judge and far fewer companies to choose from, so they select. The Series B is where selection starts in earnest. whoisnnamdi.com · “Why Don’t VCs Index Invest?”

Series B rounds halved, then came back

Median Series B primary round size peaked at $34M in Q4 2021, fell to $16M by Q1 2023, then rebounded. Bridges at B mostly sat near $8M.

Carta · median Series B primary vs. bridge round size · Q1 2020–Q1 2024

The wait for a B is now ~2.4 years

Median years between primary rounds. Both gaps have stretched by about a third since 2018 — and A → B is now the longer wait.

Carta · 11,413 US startups raising priced seed or Series A rounds · 2018–2024

23%
What the founders still own after the B. The median founding team holds 23% of the company once its Series B closes. A quarter hold less than 13%; only one team in ten still holds more than 43%. That is the stake equity refreshers and founder secondaries — the second half of tonight — get negotiated against. Carta · founding-team ownership by stage · rounds raised 2021–2025

The price — what a B costs, and what the valuation actually measuresTEAL = EXPLAINED · CLAY = SERIES B & THE TAIL

What a Series B looks like in 2025

The median B prices at 2.6× the median A ($152M vs. $58.6M post-money), raises $25M, and sells 14.5% of the company — less than the A, on a much larger base.

Carta · 2,369 rounds raised by US software startups · Jan–Sept 2025

Growth and profitability explain only about half of a software valuation

Spread in public software multiples (total) vs. the part growth and profitability explain (explained), 2020–22. Financials “never explain much more than half”; adding the macro economy lifts it to ~80% at the peak.

Nnamdi Iregbulem · whoisnnamdi.com · “The Dark Matter of Software Valuations” · Jul 2022

Same financials, very different prices

Most trade at unremarkable multiples; a few sit far out in the tail. Controlling for financials, the 90th percentile traded ~75% above prediction, the 10th ~50% below.

whoisnnamdi.com · “Dark Matter” essay · Jul 2022

Customer revenue is fat-tailed too

Power laws fit to 13 public software companies’ customer revenue all had α below 2. At α = 1.25 the top 20% bring ~72% of revenue, the top 1% ~40%.

whoisnnamdi.com · “Monetization is Fat-Tailed” · Oct 2020
