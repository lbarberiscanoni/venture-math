4 · Liquidity before the exit

Tender offers are back, and employees sell more than they did in 2021

Tenders by startups on Carta, and employees who sold

$22M median 2025 tender, at a 0% median discount to the last round. Series C+ holders who sold: 38% → 66% (2021 to H1 ’25).

How to design a tender

  1. Pick a rhythm. Staff will expect another one, at no lower a price.
  2. Decide who sells. Current staff first, former staff later.
  3. Cap it. Median caps are 20–25% of a holder’s shares.
  4. Don’t make quitting the exit. Pre-IPO Facebook barred current staff from selling, so some quit to sell.
