Venture Math

What the numbers really look like, from formation to IPO.

Founders negotiate equity, dilution and terms mostly in the dark — the benchmarks that would tell them what "normal" looks like are the one thing nobody in the room shares. This course walks the startup lifecycle stage by stage against data on 40,000+ startups, and each week a stage-appropriate investor joins to say what the numbers look like from their side of the table.
